Feeds & updates

Control feed refresh frequency and background activity.

When a feed cannot load directly, the reader tries the site's own WordPress API first, then public feed-to-JSON and reader services as a last resort. Those services see the feed or article address.

Background refresh and notifications work only where the browser allows them (Chrome on Android, installed app), at most every 12 hours, and only article text is kept offline.
